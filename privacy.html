Kando Meta — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who uses this app

Kando Meta is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Kandotv (@IAMKANDO), Doğukan Saçır, to manage his own channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission (YouTube scope youtube.force-ssl), the app:

  • reads the list of videos on the owner's own channel and their titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status and processing status;
  • updates the title, description, tags, category and translations of the owner's own unlisted videos;
  • uploads caption tracks and posts a comment on the owner's own videos;
  • reads and updates the owner's own channel description and keywords when he asks for it.

It does not access any other user's account, private data or channel.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Video audio is transcribed locally on the owner's computer.
  • To write the text, transcripts and video titles of the owner's own videos are sent to AI services the owner uses: Anthropic (Claude), OpenRouter, Google Gemini and a local Ollama model. Nothing else is sent to them.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local files can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.
